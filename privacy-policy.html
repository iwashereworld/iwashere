Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026  ·  IWASHEREWORLD  ·  sametbasinli@gmail.com

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Who We Are

I Was Here is operated by IWASHEREWORLD. For privacy-related questions, contact us at sametbasinli@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use the platform:

  • Account data: Email address, display name, and authentication data handled by Supabase Auth.
  • Location data: Geographic coordinates you choose when placing a mark.
  • Content data: Messages, photos, and recipient email fields you choose to submit.
  • Usage data: Device, browser, pages viewed, and basic interaction data.
  • Technical data: IP address and related request metadata processed by our infrastructure providers.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate and improve the I Was Here platform.
  • To display marks and related content in the product.
  • To support account features and currently available capsule features.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Supabase and other infrastructure providers we use to run the service. Password handling is managed by Supabase Auth. Access controls may be applied at the database or application level where configured.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to object to certain processing.

To make a privacy request, email us at sametbasinli@gmail.com.

6. Cookies

We use cookies or similar browser storage for login state, preferences, and core product functionality. Some third-party services we use may also process technical request data when their resources are loaded.

7. Third-Party Services

We currently rely on third-party services including:

  • Supabase - database and authentication (privacy policy)
  • Cesium Ion - 3D globe rendering (privacy policy)
  • Vercel - website hosting (privacy policy)

8. Data Retention

We keep data for as long as needed to operate the platform, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You can contact us to request account deletion.

9. Children's Privacy

I Was Here is not intended for children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us.

10. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your own depending on the infrastructure providers we use.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of the page reflects the latest revision.
